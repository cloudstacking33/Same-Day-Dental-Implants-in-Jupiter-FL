Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about implants and our practice.

What are same day dental implants?

Same day dental implants allow implant placement and attachment of temporary or fixed teeth during one visit. Not every patient qualifies, so a consultation and imaging review come first.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with good general health and enough jawbone can be candidates. If bone is thin or gum disease is present, treatment such as grafting or periodontal care may be recommended first.

How long do dental implants last?

With daily cleaning, regular checkups, and healthy gums, titanium implants are designed to last many years and often decades. The crown on top may need replacement over time.

Can failed dental implants be fixed?

Often yes. Depending on the cause, treatment may involve infection control, bone support, implant revision, or removal and replacement. A detailed evaluation shows the best path.

A little preparation makes your visit more productive. Write down your questions and any symptoms you have noticed, such as pain, swelling, or difficulty chewing. Bring a list of your current medications and any relevant medical information, since your general health affects how you heal after implant surgery.

If you have a denture or bridge, you are welcome to bring it with you. Seeing how it fits helps us understand your needs. You may also want to think about your goals for appearance and function, such as eating certain foods again or feeling comfortable smiling in photos.
